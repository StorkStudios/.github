# Stork'Studios
We are Stork’Studios, a small team of young developers who want to create special and amazing games. Currently we’re focusing on our main project: Bullet Waste, a combination of first person slasher and bullet hell. We also love to participate in game jams and share our creations with others. You can find more about us and our projects on [our website](https://storkstudios.github.io/).

## Our repositories
We publish code of the games we make during various game jams. At our GitHub profile you can also find [CoreNest](https://github.com/StorkStudios/CoreNest) - our package of scripts supporting development of Unity games.
